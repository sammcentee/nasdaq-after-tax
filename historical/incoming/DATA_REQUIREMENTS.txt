Data handoff for the Nasdaq-100 direct-share / Irish-tax comparison
================================================================

You do not need to collect this manually. Public filings, issuer notices,
historical fund holdings and market-data sources can supply many of the gaps.
This document describes useful exports if you already have access to a dataset.

How to supply data
------------------
Place original CSV, XLSX, Parquet or ZIP exports in this directory, or attach
them in the conversation. Provide the provider/dataset name, download date and
field definitions, especially the price-adjustment convention. Original native
exports are preferred; no manual renaming or conversion is required. A small
sample and the provider's documentation are enough to assess coverage first.
PDF filings or links to original issuer/exchange announcements also help.

Core period: 2010-09-30 through 2026-09-30 inclusive. Include earlier observations
back to 2009-09-30 if available, to drift the earliest archived holdings forward.
Universe: every stock held by the Nasdaq-100 during the core period, plus all
successor securities and spin-offs that a holder would receive and retain.
Continue each price history after index deletion; include delisted/failed firms.

1. Dated index membership and target weights
------------------------------------------
Daily or rebalance-date Nasdaq-100 constituent weights (or index share counts
and corresponding prices), stable security identifiers, effective date and the
date/time the information became publicly available. Archived full QQQ/CNDX
holdings are a useful alternative. Membership alone does not supply weights.
Do not substitute today's constituents or interpolate using a future snapshot.

2. Prices, dividends and identifiers
-----------------------------------
Daily unadjusted closing prices with currency, stable security identifiers,
dated ticker/name/ISIN mappings and share-class identification. If only adjusted
prices are supplied, include exact adjustment factors and definitions. Gross
cash dividends need amount, currency, ex-date and payment date. Include split
ratios and effective dates. Total-return adjusted prices alone cannot establish
the historical taxable dividends or acquisition/disposal amounts.

3. Corporate actions and terminal outcomes
------------------------------------------
Cash takeover consideration per share, successor security IDs and exchange
ratios, spin-off distributions, effective/settlement dates, fractional-share
cash terms, and any contingent rights or liquidation payments. Preserve links
to the issuer/SEC/exchange documents. A last available price is not necessarily
the cash received. A price adjustment is not a substitute for successor holdings.

Tax treatment and euro acquisition-basis allocation must be checked separately
for Irish investors. A US tax-basis form alone does not determine Irish treatment.
Mixed cash/share mergers also need an extension to the existing stock engine;
receiving a dataset will not automatically produce a validated final result.

Already available in this project
--------------------------------
Official CNDX NAV history and ECB exchange rates, current Trading 212 instrument
metadata and fee assumptions, 154 archived weight snapshots, preliminary stock
histories, the tax-lot engines and audits. Remaining work includes validating and
joining these sources; no additional personal broker history is required for
the hypothetical EUR 1,000/month strategy.

Provider checks before spending money
-------------------------------------
Nasdaq Global Index Watch advertises historical components and weights. Confirm
the export entitlement, start date and publication/announcement fields cover
this exact period; the public product page does not establish all of those.
https://www.nasdaq.com/products/global-indexes/data/giw

Norgate supplies raw/adjusted and delisted price histories and historical index
membership in relevant tiers. Its FAQ explicitly excludes merger consideration
and successor details, so it is not a complete replacement for the event ledger.
Its documented workflow uses a Windows updater; native exports can be assessed
here. Confirm the licence allows the intended export and use.
https://norgatedata.com/data-content-tables.php
https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php

Trading 212's documented historical endpoints concern account orders, dividends
and transactions; no documented 16-year constituent price/corporate-action feed
was found. A second broker key or demo account would not fill these data gaps.
https://docs.trading212.com/api

Public SEC submissions data requires no API key. Issuer filings and exchange
notices can resolve identities, cash consideration and successor terms.
https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces
